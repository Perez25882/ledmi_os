# LedgerMind OS — Cashier & Manager Training Guide

## 1. Quick start for cashiers

### Opening your shift
1. Tap the **shift icon** on the home screen.
2. Enter the cash float in the drawer.
3. Tap **Open Shift**.

### Making a sale
1. Scan the product barcode or tap **Search**.
2. Adjust quantity if needed.
3. Tap **Checkout**.
4. Choose payment method:
   - **Cash**: enter amount tendered and confirm.
   - **Card/MoMo**: tap the button, then complete payment on the customer phone or Paystack page.
5. The receipt prints automatically.

### Processing a refund
1. Tap the **refund icon** on the home screen.
2. Enter the **Sale ID** from the receipt and tap **Lookup**.
3. Select the quantity to refund for each item.
4. Add a reason and tap **Process Refund**.
5. The inventory is returned to stock automatically.

### Closing your shift
1. Tap the **shift icon**.
2. Review sales summary.
3. Tap **Close Shift** and enter the actual cash counted.
4. If there is a discrepancy, tell the manager immediately.

## 2. Manager dashboard overview

### Overview page
- See today's revenue, sales count, and branch performance.
- Enable browser alerts with the **Enable Alerts** button.
- Stock alerts highlight items that need reordering.

### Inventory
- Live stock for the selected branch.
- Updates in real time when sales or refunds happen.

### Catalog
- Add new products, categories, SKUs, and reorder levels.
- Delete products to archive them.

### Sales & Refunds
- Filter sales by date range.
- Export sales to CSV.
- Review refund history.

### Shifts
- See every cashier shift with opening float, expected cash, actual cash, and discrepancy.

### Ledmi AI
- Ask natural-language questions like:
  - "What should I reorder today?"
  - "Which products have low margin?"
  - "What were today's top products?"

## 3. Best practices

- Always open a shift before selling.
- Verify the branch is correct before checkout.
- Keep the device charged and connected when possible.
- Do not store full card numbers or CVV anywhere.
- Report discrepancies before closing a shift.
- Use the refund flow instead of editing inventory directly.

## 4. Offline mode

If the network is down:
- Cash sales are saved locally and synced automatically when the connection returns.
- Card/MoMo payments require internet and cannot be queued.
- Do not uninstall the app before the queue is empty.
