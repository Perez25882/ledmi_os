The Problem

$500B+ in African retail is managed with guesswork

Unreliable connectivity

Cloud-only POS systems stop working when data drops — lost sales, every day, across the continent.

No multi-branch visibility

Chain owners can't see stock, sales, or margins across outlets in real time. Decisions are days late.

No predictive intelligence

Existing "AI" in African POS is dashboards and low-stock alerts. No one predicts stockouts or recommends reorders.

Pricing mismatch

Market split between too-simple (SellarPro, GHS 99/mo) and too-expensive (Oval POS, $120–150/outlet/mo). Mid-market chain underserved.

Business model & unit economics

SaaS + payments share. ~90% gross margin.

  • SaaS: GHS 99 / 199 / 299 per outlet/mo (Starter / Growth / Chain). Growth is the core ICP tier.
  • Payments share: 15–25% of Paystack fees on transactions we route — pure margin, costs the merchant nothing extra.
  • Optional platform fee: GHS 0.10/non-cash txn, capped at GHS 50/outlet/mo. No fee on cash.
  • 5-outlet chain (illustrative): ~GHS 3,245/mo (~$216) total revenue; marginal cost <GHS 300; ~90%+ gross margin; CAC payback 1–2 months.

A 5-outlet chain pays Oval POS $600–750/mo. They pay us ~$216/mo for more capability. The switch is a no-brainer.
